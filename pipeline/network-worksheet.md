# The 250-name network list — recall worksheet

The hard part isn't the format, it's remembering everyone. This turns "think of 250 people"
into "name four or five from each bucket." Work through it in one sitting; don't stop to
research anyone or decide whether they're worth including. Get names down, judge later.

Put the names in `pipeline/network.csv` as you go, or fill this in and transfer after.

## Tiers

| Tier | Who | Target | How you reach them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Would take your call today | ~40 | Phone or coffee. Never a message. |
| **T2** | Know you well, haven't spoken in a while | ~110 | Personal email, LinkedIn DM as backup |
| **T3** | Looser ties, would remember you | ~100 | LinkedIn DM |

---

## By company — the main engine

For **each** company below, list names under every bucket that applies. Most companies
should produce 15–35 names. Eight companies gets you to roughly 200.

**Companies:** Sobrynth · VIVIO Health · You Are Accountable · Koa Health · Spring Health ·
BI WORLDWIDE · Dealer Product Services · Ferrandino & Son · Prudential Douglas Elliman

Buckets, per company:

- [ ] **Your manager, and their manager**
- [ ] **Peers on your team** — the people in the same meetings every week
- [ ] **People who reported to you** ← *the highest-value bucket on this page. Anyone who
      reported to you at BI WORLDWIDE five to eight years ago is a director or VP somewhere
      now. Warm, senior, and they remember who taught them.*
- [ ] **Cross-functional partners** — marketing, product, CS, ops, finance, legal, RevOps
- [ ] **Customers and clients you sold to** — especially champions who've since moved
- [ ] **Partners, channel, vendors** — including anyone who sold *to* you
- [ ] **People who left for somewhere interesting** — the diaspora is your reach multiplier

## Beyond the résumé

- [ ] **Recruiters** — anyone who placed you, pitched you, or you turned down. They see
      founder-led-sales pain constantly and it's the referral swap in the playbook.
- [ ] **Investors and VC platform people** you've met through any of the five health roles
- [ ] **Founders who've asked your advice** informally — you've probably done this for free
      more than once
- [ ] **Conference and industry contacts** — HLTH, ViVE, StartUp Health, benefits conferences
- [ ] **Hofstra** — alumni in health, benefits, or operator roles
- [ ] **Recovery and advocacy community** — people who know your work in this space
- [ ] **Shit Moms Won't Say** — collaborators, guests, people who became real relationships
- [ ] **Anyone who commented on or reposted your launch post** — they already raised a hand
- [ ] **Anyone who's viewed your profile in the last week** — check before it decays

## Rules while you work

1. **Don't self-censor.** "They wouldn't remember me" is almost always wrong, and a T3 name
   costs you nothing to have written down.
2. **Don't research.** No LinkedIn rabbit holes. Name, company, tier, done.
3. **Tier by warmth, not seniority.** A peer who'd pick up beats a CEO who wouldn't.
4. **T1 needs a reason.** For every T1, write one specific line: why you're reaching out to
   *them*, not a generic hello. That line is the whole message.

## When it's built

Work T1 first — 10 calls beat 100 emails. Then T2 at 20/week for the first twelve weeks,
then 5/week as maintenance. Log every touch in `pipeline/touches.csv` so channel attribution
is real data rather than a guess.
