For Jess's VIVIO Health seat (Sr. Director of Sales), not the Carietti practice.

## The reposition

PE doesn't buy "pharmacy risk management." PE buys three things, and pharmacy touches all of them:

1. **EBITDA.** Specialty is the fastest-compounding line in a portfolio company's benefits
   spend, and it sits in a contract signed by whoever ran HR before the deal closed. Nobody
   re-priced it at close.
2. **Portfolio-wide leverage.** One decision applied across 8–30 companies, and applied again
   at every add-on, is the only kind of cost work a PE operating partner actually likes.
3. **Fiduciary exposure.** The ERISA plan-sponsor suits land on named fiduciaries at the
   portco — often people the sponsor placed. "No fiduciary risk" is already VIVIO's own
   positioning; with this audience it is the lead, not a footnote.

Captives stay in the message (it's SIIA), but as a *structure* the reader already knows,
not as the thing being sold.

## What changed from the original, and why

| Original | Why it was cut |
|---|---|
| "wanted to reach out to see if there's an opportunity to connect" | Filler, and it's the line that signals mass send. The ask is now the last two sentences. |
| "VIVIO works with captive pools to manage pharmacy risk collectively" | True, but it opens with VIVIO. The first sentence has to be about the reader. |
| "building a model around reducing cost while actually improving outcomes" | Every vendor at SIIA says this. Deleted. |
| "Structurally, VIVIO's PMPM-only model with… is how we operate" | Kept — it's the only genuinely differentiating paragraph — but compressed and given a consequence: *none of our revenue moves when a drug price moves.* |
| "built for every member's needs" | Marketing register. Replaced with portfolio applicability, which is what this reader is actually testing for. |
| "Would love to hear how you're thinking about it on your end" | Vague. Nothing to answer. |
| "Let me know availability at your convenience" | Passive, and the register doesn't match the rest. Replaced with a direct ask plus a clean way to decline. |

## Before sending — verify

- **The ERISA framing.** It's accurate as to plan sponsors and named fiduciaries, and it's
  consistent with VIVIO's own "no fiduciary risk" positioning, but it should not be stretched
  into a claim about personal liability for PE partners. Worth a sanity check with VIVIO's
  marketing or counsel before it goes out at volume.
- **The 2:1 ROI guarantee and 100% rebate pass-through** are carried over verbatim from the
  original — confirm they're still the current contractual terms.
- **Which PE reader you have.** The two drafts are not interchangeable. If the name on the
  list is an operating partner or a human capital lead, use the primary. If they're a deal
  professional covering insurance services, use the variant.
- Kill-list clean, no invented statistics, no company name in the subject.
