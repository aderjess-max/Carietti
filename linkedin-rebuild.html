Cold LinkedIn outreach

Connection notes cap at 300 characters, the recipient can see you viewed their profile, and anything long reads as automation. So the note earns the connection; the pitch waits for the accept, which is a yes.

You have an advantage almost nobody has here: in behavioral health, one line about your own recovery does more work than any credential. Use it once, early, and never as a sales move.

[Name] — saw you're hiring AEs with no sales leader in place yet. That gap is the thing I usually get called for. I've sold behavioral health for eight years — Spring Health, Koa, You Are Accountable. Not pitching you, happy to just connect.
