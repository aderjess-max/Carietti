## About — ~1,780 / 2,000 chars

The Carietti Group takes digital health companies out of founder-led sales and leaves them with a sales organization that runs without the founder.

Six months. Then we hire the VP of Sales who takes it over.

THE PROBLEM

A founder builds something that works. They sell it brilliantly, because nobody believes in it the way they do. Somewhere between $2M and $10M that stops scaling, so they hire reps — and the reps inherit a process that exists nowhere except the founder's head. Most of them wash out. It reads as a hiring problem. It's a system problem.

WHAT AN ENGAGEMENT INCLUDES

· ICP and segmentation, sized and sourced into a working target list
· Sales narrative, pitch, objection handling, proof architecture
· Email templates and multi-channel outreach cadences
· Sales process, stage definitions, qualification and forecast discipline
· Tech stack selection and implementation
· CRM configured, live, and actually used
· Onboarding and ramp plan for the reps who follow
· VP of Sales search — scorecard, comp plan, interview loop, and hire

Everything built during the engagement transfers to the client at the end.

WHO WE WORK WITH

Digital health companies between $2M and $10M — mental health, substance use, recovery, benefits, care delivery — selling to employers, health plans and providers.

HOW WE WORK

This is a practice, not an agency. You work directly with the person doing the work. No bench, no junior associate behind a senior name on the proposal, and no engagement that runs longer than it should.

We take a small number of engagements at a time. The work is hands-on and it doesn't survive being spread thin.

Led by Jess Ferretti — 23 years in sales leadership, quotas carried to $12M, and eight years selling digital health at Spring Health, Koa Health, You Are Accountable, VIVIO Health and Sobrynth.

## What this page is for

Company pages get roughly 5% of feed allocation against ~65% for personal profiles, so this is
**a credential page, not a channel.** Its jobs: look real to a procurement reviewer, be a
sensible destination when someone clicks the company name on Jess's profile, and hold the
entity's identity.

Don't try to run content from it. Reshare a personal post to the page once or twice a month so
it isn't visibly dormant, and leave it there.
