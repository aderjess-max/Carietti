# Channel 1 — LinkedIn

**Audience**: CHROs, HR Directors, Benefits Leaders, EAP decision-makers, Workplace Culture and DEI
leaders, benefits brokers and consultants.

**What they care about**: Reducing presenteeism and absenteeism costs. Talent attraction and
retention. Psychological safety. Inclusive culture. Staying ahead of workforce health trends.
Benefits that go beyond checkbox EAP culture.

**Cadence**: 1 post per week, published Tuesday or Wednesday morning.

**Format**: Text-only, or text with a single stat. Occasional article links — once a month maximum.
150–300 words. Never more than 400.

## The post formula

Every post follows this four-part structure.

1. **Hook (line 1)** — A striking stat, bold claim, unexpected question, or short story that earns
   the "see more" click. This is the only line visible before truncation. It must stop the scroll.
2. **Body (2–4 short paragraphs)** — Frame the problem. Provide the insight. Use data where
   possible. No more than 2 industry terms per post, always explained.
3. **Pivot** — Shift from problem to possibility. Give them a path forward. Position Sobrynth as
   part of that path without a hard pitch.
4. **Close** — A question, a reflection, or a gentle invitation to connect. No hard sells.

## The 5 content pillars

Rotate through these each month.

| Pillar | What it does |
|---|---|
| **The Business Case** | Data and ROI. Cost of untreated SUD, productivity, absenteeism, the gap in commercial employer benefits. |
| **The Culture Shift** | Inclusive culture, belonging, what alcohol-centric work rituals communicate, what recovery-friendly actually means. |
| **The Workforce Trend** | Gen Z sober-curious movement, shifting workplace expectations, forward-thinking HR leadership. |
| **The Human Story** | Brief, emotionally resonant posts that humanize SUD in the workplace and break stigma in the feed. |
| **The Solution Frame** | What good looks like. Peer recovery coaches, 24/7 support, 90-day culture programs — described without pitching. |
