# Buying signals and scoring

## The signals, ranked

Ranked by how well each predicts a real conversation. Signal 1 outranks the rest combined —
when in doubt, spend the week's effort there.

| # | Signal | Points | How to verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hiring AEs or SDRs with **no** VP/Head of Sales/CRO on the team | 40 | Job board posting is live AND a LinkedIn people search on the company returns no sales leadership title |
| 2 | Head of Sales / VP Sales req open more than 60 days | 30 | Posting date on the listing, or a re-post of the same role |
| 3 | Series A raised 9–24 months ago | 20 | Funding announcement date. Under 9 months is too early; over 30 is usually resolved or stalled |
| 4 | First sales hire departed inside 18 months | 25 | LinkedIn tenure check on the company's sales alumni |
| 5 | Founder still the contact on demo/pricing/contact page | 15 | The company's own website |
| 6 | New segment, geography, or product announced in last 6 months | 15 | Press releases, LinkedIn company posts, changelog |

## Scoring

Sum the points for every signal that hits, then apply modifiers:

**Add:**
- `+15` in-vertical (digital health or construction tech / commercial construction)
- `+10` headcount 15–150
- `+10` founder/CEO does **not** have a sales background — they know it's not their strength
- `+5` NY metro (in-person option is a real closing advantage)
- `+10` a warm path exists — shared connection, investor you know, prior colleague

**Subtract:**
- `−20` already has a VP Sales, CRO, or Head of Sales in seat
- `−15` headcount over 250 — likely past the founder-led ceiling
- `−15` raised Series B or later
- `−10` pre-revenue or under ~$1M — can't carry $12K/month
- `−25` no verifiable observation available after research

## Thresholds

| Score | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 70+ | `hot` | Research deeply, personalize hard, consider a warm path before going cold |
| 55–69 | `active` | Standard four-touch sequence |
| 40–54 | `nurture` | One touch, then park. Re-score in 90 days |
| under 40 | `discard` | Do not add to pipeline |

## Verticals

**Digital health** — provider-facing, payer-facing, or employer-facing software and services.
Care coordination, clinical ops, RCM, patient access, mental health and substance use, specialty
pharmacy and benefits, clinical research infrastructure. The tell for fit: a long committee sale
with a compliance gate.

Always call the category **digital health** in any drafted copy. Never write "behavioral health"
as the label — where depth needs showing, name the specifics (mental health, substance use,
recovery) instead.

**Construction tech** — software and services sold to GCs, subs, owners, developers,
suppliers. Preconstruction, project management, field ops, permitting, materials, equipment,
construction finance.

**Construction owner-operators** — a separate and less contested target class. ENR-ranked
regional GCs, specialty subcontractors, building-products manufacturers roughly $20–200M
revenue, typically family- or founder-owned, owner still the top salesperson. Score these on
signals 5 and 6 plus revenue scale; the funding signals don't apply. Use Play 3 CT variant.

## The weekly search

Run this Monday morning, both verticals:

1. Job boards — Wellfound, Built In, YC jobs, LinkedIn — for `Account Executive`,
   `Sales Development`, `Enterprise Sales` at companies 15–150 headcount in either vertical.
2. For each hit, check LinkedIn people for `VP Sales`, `Head of Sales`, `CRO`,
   `Chief Revenue`. **No result is the signal.**
3. Cross-reference recent funding: AlleyWatch, Tech:NYC trackers, Bricks & Bytes (contech),
   Rock Health and MedCity (digital health).
4. Score, keep everything 40+, discard the rest without sentiment.

Target: 15 qualified companies per week. Quality over quota — report a short list honestly
rather than padding it.
