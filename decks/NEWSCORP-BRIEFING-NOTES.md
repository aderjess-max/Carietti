## Sources for the media facts slide

1. **3rd of 19 industries / 12.9% vs 9.5%** — SAMHSA NSDUH, *Substance Use and
   Substance Use Disorder by Industry* (arts, entertainment and recreation).
2. **13.7% past-month illicit drug use**, 2nd highest of any industry vs 8.6% — same source.
3. **86–100% trauma exposure; PTSD 4–13%; 28.6% lifetime in war correspondents** —
   Feinstein et al. 2002; Dart Center for Journalism and Trauma.
4. **8 in 10 burnout / 38% declining mental health / half considered leaving** —
   Media Resilience Network national survey 2026; Muck Rack *State of Journalism* 2025.
5. **25% more alcohol among night-shift workers** — *Shiftwork and Alcohol Consumption:
   A Systematic Review*, European Addiction Research.
6. **17,163 media job cuts in 2025, up 15%** — Challenger, Gray & Christmas 2025.
